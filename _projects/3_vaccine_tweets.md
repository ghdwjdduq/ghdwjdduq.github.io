A group course project for COMP 484 at Macalester College, with Prof. Susan Fox. We annotated tweets about COVID-19 vaccines into government conspiracy, medical freedom, and medical risk, then fine-tuned DistilBERT. The write-up reports about 75% accuracy on the annotated set.
