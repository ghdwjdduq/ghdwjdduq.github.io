Built a medical ontology for a multimodal chatbot that simulates people with different levels of ability in activities of daily living. The agent is used to train professional assessors. Mentor: Serguei Pakhomov.

[System description (Finzel et al., EACL 2021)](https://aclanthology.org/2021.eacl-demos.38/)
