University of Washington

I am a Ph.D. student in computational linguistics at the University of Washington. I find joy in expressions that are hard to translate, and I love reading and writing fiction. I believe anyone can be a writer, even a computer, and I study natural language processing — text generation and linguistic bias — to test that idea.

My research interests are natural language generation, semantic representation, ethics in AI, linguistic bias, and multilingual NLP.

I studied linguistics and computer science at Macalester College. Advisors: Christina Esposito and Susan Fox.
