I am a Ph.D. student in computational linguistics at the [University of Washington](https://www.washington.edu/). I find joy in expressions that are hard to translate, and I love reading and writing fiction. I believe anyone can be a writer, even a computer, and I study [natural language processing](/projects/) — text generation and linguistic bias — to test that idea.

My research interests are natural language generation, semantic representation, ethics in AI, linguistic bias, and multilingual NLP.

I studied linguistics and computer science at [Macalester College](https://www.macalester.edu/). Advisors: Christina Esposito and Susan Fox.
