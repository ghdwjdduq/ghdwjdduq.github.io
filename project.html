Conversational Agent for Daily Living Assessment Coaching

University of Minnesota · 2020 · Mentor: Serguei Pakhomov

Built a medical ontology for a multimodal chatbot that simulates people with different levels of ability in activities of daily living. The agent is used to train professional assessors who interview people about daily functioning.

System description (Finzel et al., EACL 2021)

Korean Abstract Meaning Representation

Yonsei Institute of Language and Information Studies · 2020 · Mentor: Hansaem Kim

Annotated sentences and wrote guidelines for a Korean Abstract Meaning Representation corpus, and built a zero-anaphora corpus in Korean.

Code · Corpus paper (Choe et al., DMR 2020)

Intent classification of COVID-19 anti-vaccination tweets

COMP 484, Macalester College · Fall 2021 · Instructor: Susan Fox

A group course project on fine-grained intent in tweets about COVID-19 vaccines. We annotated tweets into government conspiracy, medical freedom, and medical risk, then fine-tuned DistilBERT. The write-up reports about 75% accuracy on the annotated set.

Code · Paper
