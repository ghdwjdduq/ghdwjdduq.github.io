About

I am a senior majoring in linguistics and computer science at Macalester College. I find joy in expressions that are hard to translate, and I love reading and writing fiction. I believe anyone can be a writer, even a computer, and I am studying natural language processing — text generation and linguistic bias — to test that idea.

My research interests are natural language generation, semantic representation, ethics in AI, linguistic bias, and multilingual NLP.
